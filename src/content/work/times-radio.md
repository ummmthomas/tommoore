## The challenge

The Times and The Sunday Times had a loyal, multigenerational subscriber base who only consumed Times journalism in written form, while spending their leisure time listening to third-party talk radio and podcasts.

## Introducing Times Radio

A news and talk station available without a subscription, designed to deepen engagement with subscribers and reach new audiences across web and native devices.

## My role

I led the UX work from concept through to implementation: validating demand, shaping the product architecture and coordinating with cross-functional teams.

## Assessing demand

Launching a radio station is expensive, so I built low-cost prototypes from workshopped user stories and ran in-person studies with both loyal subscribers and non-subscribers. Analysing the quantitative and qualitative data surfaced three key insights that supported the case for further investment.

## Architectural design

The architecture drew on user research, information hierarchy, wireframing and iterative testing. It prioritised easy navigation and quick access to the player and on-demand content, and recommended related editorial content to encourage listening among non-subscribers. The web architecture fitted the existing and planned editorial structure, with a similar map created for the native apps.

## Outcomes

- **Weekly listening hours up 25%** year on year, reaching a record **3.96 million hours** in Q4 2023.
- **600k weekly listeners.**
- Strongly positive feedback, with high satisfaction and Net Promoter Score.

Former Prime Minister Boris Johnson was the inaugural guest in the studio on launch day.

## Learnings

- **Understand technical constraints early.** Design acted as a mediator between siloed teams, giving Product, Design and Engineering clear mental models of possible scopes so everyone agreed on release expectations.
- **De-risk.** Testing the concept directly with users helped the company allocate resources based on expected return on investment.
